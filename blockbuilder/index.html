An AI that sees your engine

Send a photo of a connector or bracket and ask what it is. The assistant knows which engine and step you are on, and tells you when to check the factory manual instead of guessing.

What is this plug next to the throttle body?

That looks like the idle air control or throttle position connector. Check it against the wiring photo you took of that area, and confirm in your service manual.

Installs like an app

Add it to your phone's home screen. It opens full screen, loads fast and keeps working under the car where signal is poor.

Guides are starter data and AI answers can be wrong. Always confirm procedures and torque specs against the factory service manual.
